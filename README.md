# Castaway

A 3D survival game built with Three.js. You're the lone survivor of a plane crash, stranded on a tropical island.

Open `index.html` in a browser (it loads Three.js from a CDN, so you need an internet connection).

## How to play

- **WASD / arrows** move, **Shift** sprint, **Space** jump, **E** interact
- **Drag** to look around, **scroll** to zoom, **M** mute, **Esc** pause
- On touch screens: left thumb moves, drag on the right to look, on-screen buttons for interact and jump

1. Search the torn-off tail section for a way to make fire
2. Collect 5 driftwood and build the signal fire at the stone ring on the beach
3. Light it, then keep feeding it wood until a ship spots your signal

Stay alive while you do it: hunger and thirst drain over time (coconuts, supply crates and suitcases help, and you can shake palms for more coconuts), nights are cold away from a fire, and don't wade too far out — there's a shark.

## Features

- Day/night cycle with physical sky, sunsets, stars, moon and drifting clouds
- Procedural island with wind-animated grass, palms, ferns and jungle trees
- Ocean shader with Gerstner waves, depth-based color, shoreline foam and caustics on the sand underwater
- Crashed airliner with fire, smoke and embers; footprints in the sand; crabs, seagulls and fireflies
- Minimap, objectives tracker, procedural sound, bloom, MSAA and color grading
